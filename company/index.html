Our Studio

A small, independent studio that builds with care.

Aphelion Software was established in 2025 with a straightforward purpose: turn useful ideas into focused, dependable software.

Our independence keeps us close to the work. We choose practical problems, build deliberately, and keep improving the products we operate. GoodTalk is one result of that approach: an iPhone app designed to help couples communicate through difficult moments with more clarity.

How we work

Thoughtful products, responsibly operated.

Good software should solve a clear problem, respect the people using it, and keep getting better after launch.

Start with a real need

We look for everyday problems where a focused product can make something meaningfully easier.

Build deliberately

We favour clear design, dependable engineering, and useful features over unnecessary complexity.

Stay accountable

We operate what we build, listen to feedback, and keep privacy, support, and maintenance part of the product.
